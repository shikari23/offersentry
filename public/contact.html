Do not email sensitive personal information

Do not send passwords, Social Security numbers, banking information, identification documents, authentication codes, or complete copies of private conversations. Remove unnecessary personal details before sharing an example.

Need to report a scam?

OfferSentry is not a law-enforcement agency and cannot investigate individual scammers. Reports in the United States can be submitted directly to the Federal Trade Commission.
