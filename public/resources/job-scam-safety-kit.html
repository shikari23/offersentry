Printable resource

One-page job-scam safety handout

Before You Accept: Job-Scam Safety Check

Pause and verify an employer before sending money, depositing a check, clicking unfamiliar links, or sharing sensitive information.

Major warning signs

  • You never applied for the position.
  • The pay is unusually high for simple work.
  • The interview happens only by text or messaging app.
  • You are hired immediately without a meaningful interview.
  • The recruiter uses a personal or lookalike email domain.
  • You receive a check and must purchase equipment.
  • You must pay, buy cryptocurrency, or send gift cards.
  • Sensitive information is requested before verification.
  • You are pressured to act before researching the offer.

Verify independently

  1. Find the company’s official website yourself.
  2. Confirm the job appears on its official careers page.
  3. Compare the recruiter’s email domain with the real website.
  4. Contact the company using information you found independently.
  5. Refuse checks, payment requests, gift cards, and cryptocurrency.
  6. Do not share passwords or verification codes.
  7. Discuss the offer with someone you trust.
Never pay to receive a job, unlock wages, continue tasks, or purchase equipment from a vendor selected by an unverified recruiter.

If you already responded

  • Stop communicating and save the evidence.
  • Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if you paid.
  • Change exposed or reused passwords.
  • Never provide a verification code.
  • Visit IdentityTheft.gov if identity information was exposed.
