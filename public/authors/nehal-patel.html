About Nehal

Nehal Patel founded OfferSentry to help job seekers recognize warning signs in suspicious recruiter messages, remote job offers, company domains, and hiring processes.

He maintains OfferSentry’s browser-based screening tools and reviews its educational guides for clarity, accuracy, usefulness, and responsible safety wording.

OfferSentry does not attempt to declare an employer definitively safe or fraudulent. Its tools and guides identify warning signs and encourage users to verify employers through independent sources.

Editorial responsibilities

  • Reviews guides before publication
  • Checks factual claims against primary sources
  • Maintains links to official reporting and recovery resources
  • Updates articles when scam patterns or guidance change
  • Reviews automated-tool language for accuracy and limitations
  • Corrects errors reported by readers

Research and sourcing

OfferSentry prioritizes primary and authoritative sources, including the Federal Trade Commission, IdentityTheft.gov, ICANN, and other official consumer-protection and internet-governance resources.

Sources and further-reading links appear at the end of each guide. Facts are summarized in original language rather than copied from the source material.

Read OfferSentry's editorial standards and tool methodology for the specific review process and the limits of automated screening.

Corrections

If you identify an error, outdated source, unclear statement, or potential content issue, contact OfferSentry with the page URL and a description of the concern.
