Three rules that stop many job scams

1

Verify independently

Find the company’s official website yourself and confirm both the position and recruiter through a separate contact method.

2

Never pay to get paid

Do not send money, deposit checks for equipment, buy gift cards, or purchase cryptocurrency to receive a job or earnings.

3

Protect sensitive information

Do not provide banking details, passwords, verification codes, or a Social Security number to an unverified recruiter.
