Your entries stay in this browser

OfferSentry does not send, upload, or save the information entered into this form. The report is generated locally on your device. Clearing or closing the page removes the form contents unless your browser retains them through its own autofill features.

Do not enter highly sensitive secrets

Do not enter passwords, verification codes, Social Security numbers, complete bank or card numbers, cryptocurrency private keys, seed phrases, or copies of identification documents. Use partial or redacted information where necessary.
