          Understanding the results

          What this lookup can tell you

          Domain-registration information can provide useful context, but it cannot prove that a website, company, recruiter, or job offer is safe. Established domains may be compromised, and legitimate organizations may use recently registered domains. For a complete walkthrough, read How to Check How Old a Website Is Before Trusting a Job Offer .

          Registration age

          A very recently registered domain deserves additional verification, especially when it is being used for recruiting, payments, or requests for personal information.

          Mail records

          MX records show whether a domain is configured to receive email. Their presence does not prove that the sender or organization is legitimate.

          Registrar and nameservers

          Public RDAP data may identify the registrar, important dates, nameservers, and registry statuses associated with a domain.

          Checking a recruiter or job offer?

          Compare the recruiter’s email domain with the company’s official website and review additional warning signs.
