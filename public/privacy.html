Privacy Policy

This policy explains how OfferSentry handles information when you use our website and job-scam screening tool.

Effective July 31, 2026

The short version

The current OfferSentry checker analyzes the text you paste directly inside your browser. The pasted message is not intentionally transmitted to or stored by OfferSentry.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the OfferSentry website located at offersentry.com, including the job-offer checker and informational pages published on the website.

2. Information you enter into the checker

The job-offer checker examines pasted text for common scam-related language patterns.

The current version performs this analysis locally in your web browser. OfferSentry does not intentionally upload or save the text you paste.

Even with this local processing, you should avoid pasting unnecessary sensitive information. Remove Social Security numbers, banking details, passwords, identification numbers, home addresses, and other confidential information before using any online screening tool.

3. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, OfferSentry’s hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information when you visit, including:

  • Your Internet Protocol address
  • Browser and device information
  • The date and time of a request
  • Requested pages and basic referral information
  • Security, diagnostic, and performance information

This information may be processed to deliver the website, prevent abuse, investigate errors, and maintain security and performance.

4. Hosting and service providers

OfferSentry is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. You can learn more by reading Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy .

5. Cookies

OfferSentry does not currently use advertising cookies or intentionally place cookies for personalized advertising.

Our infrastructure provider may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies for security, traffic management, and fraud prevention.

If OfferSentry adds analytics, advertising, or other cookie-based services, this policy will be updated and any legally required consent controls will be added.

6. Advertising

OfferSentry does not currently display third-party advertisements. If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated to identify relevant advertising partners and explain applicable choices regarding cookies and personalized advertising.

7. Third-party links

OfferSentry links to independent websites, such as government reporting services. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and practices. OfferSentry is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

8. Data sharing

OfferSentry does not sell the text entered into its checker. We may share limited technical information when necessary to:

  • Operate, host, and secure the website
  • Comply with applicable law or valid legal requests
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or threats to users and the service
  • Protect the rights and safety of OfferSentry and others

9. Data retention

OfferSentry does not intentionally retain the job or recruiter messages entered into the current checker.

Technical logs maintained by infrastructure providers may be retained according to their security, operational, and legal requirements.

10. Children’s privacy

OfferSentry is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect the website. However, no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when OfferSentry’s features, service providers, advertising practices, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.

13. Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the OfferSentry Contact page once it is available. Please do not include passwords, banking details, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information in a privacy inquiry.
