Help keep job-scam safety resources available

Support OfferSentry

OfferSentry provides free, privacy-conscious tools and educational resources that help job seekers recognize warning signs and verify suspicious employment opportunities.

What voluntary support helps fund

Support helps maintain the website, improve existing safety tools, research emerging scam tactics, and create practical resources for students, job seekers, educators, and workforce organizations.

Maintain free tools

Keep OfferSentry’s message checker, recruiter checker, domain tools, checklist, and reporting resources available without requiring an account.

Publish safety guides

Create source-backed educational articles about fake recruiters, payment schemes, identity theft attempts, and emerging job scams.

Improve accessibility

Refine mobile layouts, plain-language explanations, navigation, and other features that make safety information easier to use.

Support community education

Develop reusable materials for schools, libraries, career centers, and workforce-readiness programs.

Support options are being prepared

OfferSentry is completing its payment and business setup. A secure support option will be added here once that process is complete.
